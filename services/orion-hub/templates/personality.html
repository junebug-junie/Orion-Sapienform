Orion's personality

Built only from choices Orion makes: what Orion picked, and what Orion passed over.

Recent choices

The last 30 choices, newest first. Before Orion picks, the market (Orion's own ranking of what is worth doing next) puts the options in order: "ranked #1" is its first pick.

Tendencies

Kinds of choice Orion keeps making. A "No" retires the tendency: Orion stops leaning on it.

Is it changing anything?

The market decides some things with Orion's tendencies counted and some without them, so the two groups can be compared.
