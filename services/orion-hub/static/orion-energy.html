What these numbers do and don't include
  • athena: its server management chip (iLO) reading at the power supply. circe and hecate: the smart power strip (PDU) at the wall. Hecate draws power even when it is "off".
  • Cabinet cooling: the portable AC on the Shelly Z-Wave plug.
  • Not measured: prometheus, the network switch, the UPS's own losses, monitors. Orion's real share is somewhat higher than shown.
  • Per-machine history starts 2026-09-22 and cooling 2026-09-26; nothing older was kept.
  • RMP data arrives about a day late and has a hole from mid-August to mid-September. A dashed house line means that period's RMP data is incomplete.
  • RMP reports each hour as a whole number of kWh. Rounding mostly cancels out over a day (about ±1.5%), but not within an hour.
  • Orion cost prices each Orion hour at the house's next-kWh rate that hour. Under block pricing, that is what Orion adds to the bill. Costs are before tax.
